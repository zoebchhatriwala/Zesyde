How This Actually Works (100% Transparent Architecture)

To avoid misleading claims, here is the exact separation of responsibilities between classical simulation and neural model routing:

  1. Candidate Physics Simulation: The engine calculates physically legal landings across rotations and columns, scoring stability based on surface bumpiness, height, and holes.
  2. Candidate Selection: The top candidate moves are formatted as typed multiple-choice options with their line clears and hole counts.
  3. Neural Decision Forward Pass: Zesyde's 150M-class foundation model runs ONNX cross-attention over the 2D ASCII board matrix and options to select the preferred placement with calibrated confidence.
